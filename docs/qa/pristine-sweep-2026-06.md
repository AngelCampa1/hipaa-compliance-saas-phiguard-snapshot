## F21 — pSEO learn pages: mangled title casing in templated boilerplate (FIXED)
The `/learn/*` content was generated by interpolating each page title into three boilerplate sentences appended to every article — "Treat {Title} as an operational control…", "For {Title}, the evidence should be practical…", "Review {Title} when the clinic changes software…". The generator lowercased the title's first letter, producing ungrammatical openers: "Treat **incidental Disclosure**", "Review **what Counts as a HIPAA Breach**", "For **designated Record Set**", plus "**new-Hire**", "**de-Identified**", "**vendor Management**", "**how to Audit**", "**when a Vendor**", "**workforce Training**", "**access Review**", "**limited Data Set**", "**what Is ePHI**". Titles starting with acronyms (PHI/HIPAA) or the article "the" were unaffected (already correct). The kind of sloppiness a buyer or an 80-yo reader notices immediately. **Fix:** targeted script capitalized only the title's first letter in those three sentence templates (never touching legitimate lowercase "the"); **60 sentences across 20 learn pages** corrected. No live generator contains the boilerplate (content is committed static `.md`, generated once in a prior session), so the fix is durable — nothing regenerates it. Verified: 0 residual mangled-title strings anywhere in `learn/`, other clusters (locations/personas/practice-types/alternatives/glossary/resources) never had the pattern, and `/learn/phi-fundamentals/incidental-disclosure` renders "Treat Incidental Disclosure…" / "Review Incidental Disclosure…" correctly.
