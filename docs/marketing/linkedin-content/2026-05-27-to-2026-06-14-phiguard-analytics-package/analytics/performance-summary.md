- All dates and times in the export are UTC.
- The `Metrics` sheet reports engagement by view date. The `All posts` sheet reports post-level performance by created date, so totals will not reconcile exactly.
- All 179 posts in `All posts` are organic. Sponsored metrics are zero in the `Metrics` sheet.

## Observed Content Patterns

- High-impression posts are concrete clinic operations topics: patient rights complaints, offboarding, end-of-day close, incident closure, generic project-tool limits, access reviews, risk findings, and specialty workflows.
- Posts framed as plain operational corrections performed better than broad product or compliance positioning. Examples include "is not a HIPAA control," "should not close before," "not just an EHR permissions project," and "starts with a process gap."
- Engagement was strongest on posts with specific small-clinic friction: shared logins, generic work-management tools, cleaning crew/vendor access, end-of-day routines, and dermatology vendor review.
- The campaign appears reach-positive but interaction-light. The export shows 7,046 page impressions and 32 engagement actions, with no reposts.
- May 16 produced the strongest engagement day with 12 engagement actions, while May 17 produced the strongest impression day with 949 impressions.

## Strategy Implications For May 27-Jun 14

- Lead with specific clinic workflow problems instead of abstract HIPAA education. The evidence favors operational topics that name a real handoff, review, or failure mode.
- Keep using sharp first lines that challenge a common shortcut, then quickly move into the practical compliance task.
- Build more around the winning topic families: patient rights, offboarding, incident response, access reviews, end-of-day routines, shared logins, vendor/subprocessor review, and generic-tool limitations.
- Treat comments and reactions as scarce signals. Do not overfit to single posts; use repeated topic clusters and compare performance across several posts.
- Include some posts designed for clicks or saves, because click volume was only 9 across the page metrics window.

## Caveats

- The post-level impression sum in `All posts` is 8,988, while the view-date `Metrics` total is 7,046. This is expected because the two sheets use different grouping logic.
- LinkedIn export data is delayed and only current through May 17, 2026.
- No paid/sponsored activity appears in the export.
- The data does not include downstream site sessions, trials, signups, or revenue. It should guide LinkedIn content direction only, not full-funnel ROI claims.
