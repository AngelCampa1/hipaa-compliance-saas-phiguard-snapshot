# PHIGuard Founder And Traction Context

Use this truth constraint for all PHIGuard marketing content in this package.

## Current State

- PHIGuard does not have clients or customer proof yet.
- Do not imply customer adoption, usage, traction, revenue, testimonials, case studies, waitlist size, or market validation.
- The product was built by an engineer/founder who saw a gap in the market: small clinics need HIPAA-aware compliance operations and task accountability, but many available tools are either generic project management systems or broader compliance/GRC platforms that do not fit the small-clinic operating reality.

## Allowed Framing

- "PHIGuard is built for..."
- "PHIGuard is designed to..."
- "PHIGuard is positioned around..."
- "The product thesis is..."
- "The gap PHIGuard is trying to solve is..."

## Avoid

- "PHIGuard helps clinics..." when it reads like observed customer impact.
- "Clinics use PHIGuard..."
- "Customers choose PHIGuard..."
- "Trusted by..."
- "Proven..."
- "Validated by..."
- Any claim that implies live customer outcomes.
