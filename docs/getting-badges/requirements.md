## SaaSHub

Source: https://www.saashub.com/services/submit

Verified rules:

- SaaSHub accepts SaaS, IaaS, PaaS, most software products and apps, mobile apps with decent websites, and category-leading services.
- SaaSHub rejects software development agencies, waitlist-only landing pages, unreleased products, products using free subdomains, and products not in English.
- Submitted products go through approval.
- Add a few relevant categories.
- Add competitors. SaaSHub says submissions without listed competitors are slowed down and pushed lower in the queue.
- Verify the product with an email address on the product domain for higher priority.
- Use a product website URL or product page on the company website.

## Product Hunt

Sources:

- https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch
- https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch

Verified requirements and fields:

- Anyone can hunt or submit their own product when ready.
- Log in, click Submit, then New Product.
- Launch can be scheduled up to 1 month ahead.
- URL should be a direct product page, homepage, app store page, or repository where relevant.
- Shortened links and tracking links are not accepted.
- Product name should be only the name, with no description or gimmicks.
- Tagline max is 60 characters.
- Description max is 500 characters.
- Choose up to 3 launch tags.
- Thumbnail is required. Recommended square size is 240x240, under 3 MB.
- Gallery requires 2 or more images. Recommended size is 1270x760.
- Video is optional. Only YouTube links are supported and the video must not be private.
- Makers need Product Hunt usernames before launch.
- Product Hunt says users need to have been in the community for at least 1 week before they can contribute a product.
- Product Hunt allows 3 shoutouts per launch.
- Pricing options are free, paid, or paid with a free trial or plan.
- Promo fields are available if offering a Product Hunt promo.
- First comment is important. Product Hunt says 70% of products that achieved Product of the Day, Week, or Month included a maker first comment.
- First comment should include key features, who it is for, story, goals, pricing or offers if relevant, and a request for feedback, not upvotes.
- Rule-of-thumb launch time is 12:01am PST because the homepage runs on a Pacific-time 24-hour cycle.

PHIGuard status:

- Prepare now, launch after assets are complete.
- Use `https://phiguard.app` as the URL.
- Product name: `PHIGuard`.
- Pricing: paid with a free trial.
- Tags to try: Health, Productivity, SaaS. If Health is not available, use Healthcare or Compliance if shown.
- Promo: LAUNCH50, only if the team wants the public Product Hunt audience using the same launch offer.

Missing before a polished launch:

- Purpose-built 1270x760 gallery cards.
- Optional but useful YouTube demo.
- Confirm maker usernames, product social handle, and that the maker account has at least 1 week of Product Hunt community history.

Rejection or performance risks:

- Do not use UTMs in the Product Hunt submit URL.
- Do not ask for upvotes in the maker comment, emails, or social posts.
- Do not launch when the team cannot reply to comments.

## G2

Sources:

- https://research.g2.com/methodology/categorization
- https://research.g2.com/methodology/research-faq
- https://legal.g2.com/community-guidelines
- https://sell.g2.com/plans
- https://documentation.g2.com/docs/fr/g2-badges

Verified rules:

- G2 lists B2B software and services.
- G2 excludes products sold only to individual consumers.
- Different tiers or editions of the same product are not separate profiles.
- Individual software products that can be purchased on their own get one profile per product.
- The G2 profile name should match the software product name on the seller website.
- Profile names should not include descriptions, marketing language, symbols, or extra keywords.
- Products are categorized based on native functionality, not just the audience or use case.
- Category evidence can include recorded demos, blog content, presentations, website pages, and other supporting evidence.
- A product can be rejected if the website is inaccessible, the product is B2C, a listing already exists, or the request is for an extension or integration that is not eligible as a separate product.
- Reviews are moderated and must come from real verified users at real verified companies who use the software.
- Vendors must not create fake reviews, discourage negative reviews, segment requests to only positive reviewers, condition incentives on positivity, or pressure reviewers.
- `Users Love Us` badge usage is available on free profiles once earned after 20 reviews with a 4.0+ average rating, subject to G2's republication requirements.
- Digital republications of earned G2 badges must backlink to the applicable G2 product or service page and meet G2 attribution requirements.
- Other report-based, milestone, and annual badge usage requires a paid G2 plan.

## BetaList

Sources:

- https://betalist.com/criteria
- https://betalist.com/terms/submissions
- https://betalist.com/support

Verified rules:

- Product should be relatively new, recently launched, private beta, or not available yet.
- Already launched weeks ago or longer, significant prior press coverage, or same functionality on another platform can make a product less suitable.
- Each startup generally has two opportunities: once pre-launch and once during launch, with at least a few weeks between them.
- BetaList features technology startups, not blogs, newsletters, online stores, consultancies, or agencies.
- Landing page must be distinct and decent-looking, with enough product information.
- Submitter must be authorized to represent the startup.
- Startup must have a working website on its own domain.
- BetaList does not accept app store links or free hosting subdomains as the main website.
- Editorial review can take up to a few weeks.
- If accepted, feature timing is often about two months, but can vary.
- Priority listing offers faster review, not guaranteed acceptance.
- BetaList may edit grammar, clarity, length, images, categories, presentation, and link attributes.

PHIGuard status:

- Conditional.
- Use only if PHIGuard can truthfully be described as recently launched or launch-stage.
- Use `https://phiguard.app`, not the app signup page, as the main website.

Rejection risks:

- If PHIGuard is too mature, BetaList may reject it.
- If the page reads like a compliance content site instead of product software, it may underperform.
- Do not submit if the site is in the middle of a major pricing or legal copy inconsistency.
