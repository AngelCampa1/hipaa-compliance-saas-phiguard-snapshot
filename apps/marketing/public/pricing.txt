# PHIGuard Pricing

Annual billing is the default public pricing display. Monthly billing remains selectable where shown.

## Essentials
- Annual price after limited first-year offer: $358/year per clinic ($30/month equivalent, paid upfront annually)
- Annual list price: $1788/year per clinic ($149/month equivalent, paid upfront annually)
- Monthly price after limited first-year offer: $36/month per clinic, paid monthly
- Monthly list price: $179/month per clinic, paid monthly
- Best fit: Best for solo and small clinics that need a signed BAA, recurring HIPAA tasks, incidents, evidence, and an auditable operating record.
- Included baseline: Recurring HIPAA task management, Compliance starter checklists, Immutable audit trail, Incident tracking, Business Associate Agreement included
- User limit: Up to 10 staff

## Clinic
- Annual price after limited first-year offer: $454/year per clinic ($38/month equivalent, paid upfront annually)
- Annual list price: $2268/year per clinic ($189/month equivalent, paid upfront annually)
- Monthly price after limited first-year offer: $46/month per clinic, paid monthly
- Monthly list price: $229/month per clinic, paid monthly
- Best fit: Best for clinics with multiple admins or providers, more handoffs, and the need to keep tasks, evidence, incidents, and calendars connected.
- Included baseline: Recurring HIPAA task management, Compliance starter checklists, Immutable audit trail, Incident tracking, Business Associate Agreement included, Calendar integrations and connected operations
- User limit: Up to 30 staff

## Group
- Annual price after limited first-year offer: $934/year per clinic ($78/month equivalent, paid upfront annually)
- Annual list price: $4668/year per clinic ($389/month equivalent, paid upfront annually)
- Monthly price after limited first-year offer: $94/month per clinic, paid monthly
- Monthly list price: $469/month per clinic, paid monthly
- Best fit: Best for group practices and multi-site organizations that need rollup reporting, policies, training, risk, vendor BAAs, and stronger governance controls.
- Included baseline: Recurring HIPAA task management, Compliance starter checklists, Immutable audit trail, Incident tracking, Business Associate Agreement included, Calendar integrations and connected operations, Multi-location reporting, Policies, training, risk assessments, and vendor BAA tracking, SOC 2 evidence and access reviews
- User limit: Up to 100 staff
