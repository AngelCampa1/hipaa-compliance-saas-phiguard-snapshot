Teamwork.com is a project management platform built around client work. Its pricing page emphasizes projects, time tracking, intake forms, capacity, utilization, budgets, profitability, CRM/accounting integrations, and advanced security on higher tiers. Its security page describes ISO 27001, SOC 2, AWS hosting, monitoring, backups, two-factor authentication, and access controls. Teamwork also publishes general activity-feed and AI documentation.

Teamwork also has a separate Teamwork Desk product, and Teamwork Desk pricing references HIPAA verification on Enterprise. That matters because a clinic should not flatten every Teamwork product into one HIPAA claim. The right question is whether the exact product, plan, contract, and workflow are covered.

PHIGuard is the better Teamwork alternative when the job is clinic compliance evidence, not client-service delivery.

## Where Teamwork Fits

Teamwork is useful when the business model is project delivery:

- Agencies managing client projects
- Professional services teams tracking billable work
- Consultants managing retainers, budgets, and utilization
- Internal teams that need project plans, time tracking, intake, and reporting
- Support teams that use Teamwork Desk for ticketing

Those are legitimate use cases. They are just different from a small clinic's HIPAA compliance program.

For a clinic, the risk is that patient-adjacent work can enter a project task, comment, file, proof, intake form, client note, or integration. Once the tool creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI for the clinic, HHS business associate guidance requires appropriate written assurances.

## The Product-Scope Question

Teamwork.com's main project-management pricing page does not present a simple clinic-specific HIPAA workflow. Teamwork Desk pricing references HIPAA verification on Enterprise. That distinction should trigger a careful vendor review, not an assumption in either direction.

Clinics should verify:

- Which Teamwork product is covered: Teamwork.com, Desk, Chat, Spaces, or a bundle
- Which plan and contract include BAA terms
- Whether tasks, comments, files, proofs, intake forms, tickets, notifications, exports, and integrations are covered
- Whether AI features, reporting tools, CRM integrations, and accounting integrations can touch PHI
- How activity history, retention, deletion, breach notification, and subprocessors are handled

That review may be reasonable for a larger healthcare organization with procurement and IT resources. It is often too much overhead for a small practice trying to manage basic compliance obligations.

## What Changes With PHIGuard

PHIGuard starts with the compliance record:

- HIPAA training assignment and completion evidence
- Risk analysis and remediation tasks
- Vendor BAA tracking and renewal review
- Policy review and workforce acknowledgment
- Incident response tasks with retained evidence
- Access review tasks for workforce and vendor accounts

The workflow is smaller than Teamwork's project-management surface by design. A clinic does not need resource utilization reports to prove that a policy acknowledgment happened. It needs a clear owner, due date, status, evidence record, and history.

## Teamwork vs PHIGuard for Clinics

| | Teamwork | PHIGuard |
|---|---|---|
| Best fit | Client work, projects, time tracking, budgets, utilization, and delivery operations | HIPAA compliance operations |
| HIPAA/BAA path | Must be verified by exact product and plan; Desk references HIPAA verification on Enterprise | BAA-backed PHIGuard compliance workflows |
| Pricing model | Per-user and enterprise tiers | Clinic-focused plans |
| PHI risk surface | Tasks, comments, files, proofs, forms, tickets, reports, and integrations | Focused compliance tasks and evidence |
| Evidence tracking | User-created project structure | Native evidence fields tied to compliance tasks |
| Audit history | General activity feeds exist, but HIPAA-specific immutable audit fit must be verified | Built around compliance task history |

Teamwork is a stronger fit when the work is client delivery. PHIGuard is a stronger fit when the work is healthcare compliance evidence.
