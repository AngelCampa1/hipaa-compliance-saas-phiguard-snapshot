Slack is a widely used business messaging platform. Its current pricing page offers Free, Pro, Business+, and Enterprise+ paths, with Enterprise+ handled through a sales motion. Slack's HIPAA help page says Enterprise plans can be configured to support HIPAA-compliant message and file collaboration when a covered entity or business associate executes a BAA, uses Enterprise Grid for PHI, and follows Slack's HIPAA requirements.

That makes Slack more nuanced than a simple "not HIPAA" answer. Slack can have a HIPAA path. The problem for small clinics is that the HIPAA path is an enterprise messaging program, not a lightweight clinic compliance workflow.

PHIGuard is the better Slack alternative when the work needs a durable compliance record rather than a chat thread.

## Where Slack Fits

Slack is useful for fast communication:

- Internal team channels
- Vendor and partner conversations through Slack Connect
- Huddles, file sharing, clips, and search
- Integrations with productivity and support tools
- General operational coordination that does not include patient information

For healthcare, the risk is that chat feels informal. A quick message can include a patient name, a visit time, a diagnosis, a referral note, a billing issue, or a screenshot. If Slack receives, maintains, or transmits PHI for the clinic, HHS business associate guidance requires the clinic to have the right written assurances in place.

## The Enterprise HIPAA Question

Slack's own HIPAA page identifies key requirements: an Enterprise plan, an executed BAA, Enterprise Grid use for PHI, and implementation of Slack's HIPAA guidelines. Slack's documentation also narrows the HIPAA-supported use case to message and file collaboration. That review should happen before staff put PHI in any Slack surface.

Clinics should verify:

- Which Slack plan and contract are covered
- Whether message content and uploaded files are the only PHI-approved surfaces, and whether other features must stay PHI-free
- Which retention, export, legal hold, and eDiscovery settings are required
- Whether notifications, app integrations, and connected AI/search features can expose PHI
- Which administrators can access workspace data and audit exports

For a small clinic, that is a large governance surface for something staff often treat as casual communication.

## What Changes With PHIGuard

PHIGuard does not try to replace every message. It replaces the compliance work that should not live in chat:

- HIPAA training assignment and completion evidence
- Vendor BAA tracking and renewal review
- Risk analysis tasks and remediation follow-up
- Policy review and workforce acknowledgment
- Incident response tasks with retained evidence
- Access review tasks across staff and vendors

The difference is record design. A Slack thread can coordinate discussion. PHIGuard creates an assigned task with evidence, status, owner, due date, and history.

## Slack vs PHIGuard for Clinics

| | Slack | PHIGuard |
|---|---|---|
| Best fit | Messaging, channels, huddles, files, and integrations | HIPAA compliance operations |
| HIPAA/BAA path | Enterprise plan plus BAA and required configuration | BAA-backed PHIGuard compliance workflows |
| Buying motion | Self-serve for lower plans; Enterprise sales for HIPAA path | Clinic-focused plans |
| PHI risk surface | Messages, files, canvases, lists, workflows, integrations, notifications | Focused compliance tasks and evidence |

Slack is a communication layer. PHIGuard is a compliance operations layer. Clinics often need both, but they should not confuse the two.
