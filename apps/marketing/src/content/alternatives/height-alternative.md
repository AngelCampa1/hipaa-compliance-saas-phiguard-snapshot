## Short Answer

Height is no longer a live project management option. Height's official site says the company decided to discontinue operations, with the final day of service listed as September 24, 2025.

That changes the searcher's real question. The issue is not whether Height is a good tool for clinics today. The issue is what a clinic should do if it used Height before shutdown, still has exported records from Height, or found Height in old "project management alternative" research while looking for a replacement.

## Migration Checklist From Height

If your clinic used Height before shutdown, do not treat the migration as only a productivity cleanup. Treat it as a compliance evidence review.

1. Export tasks, comments, attachments, lists, and project history available from the workspace.
2. Identify any task titles, descriptions, comments, files, or integrations that may contain PHI.
3. Preserve records needed for incident, vendor, training, access, risk, or policy evidence.
4. Document Height's discontinued-vendor status in your vendor inventory.
5. Close or migrate open tasks with owners and due dates.
6. Move future PHI-bearing and compliance workflows into a BAA-covered system.
7. Review where exports are stored so PHI is not left in personal drives, email attachments, or unmanaged folders.

## Why This Matters for Clinics

A discontinued project tool can still matter during an audit or incident review. If a clinic used Height to track patient-adjacent tasks, the historical record may show who knew about an issue, who owned follow-up, and whether the clinic completed corrective action.

The clinic needs to preserve that evidence without continuing to rely on a vendor that is no longer operating.

## What Changes With PHIGuard

PHIGuard is not an autonomous project management replacement for every team. It is a HIPAA-focused workspace for the work a clinic needs to keep current and provable:

- vendor BAA tracking
- incident response
- risk remediation
- training evidence
- policy review
- access review
- audit history

That makes PHIGuard the right replacement for the Height workflows that should never have lived in a generic project board: PHI-bearing tasks, compliance follow-up, incident records, vendor evidence, and policy/training ownership.

## Recommendation

If your team liked Height for general product or operations work, choose a new generic project management tool for non-PHI projects.

If Height contained patient-adjacent or compliance work, migrate that work separately. Export and preserve the historical record, document the vendor shutdown, and move the future workflow into PHIGuard or another BAA-covered HIPAA operations system.
