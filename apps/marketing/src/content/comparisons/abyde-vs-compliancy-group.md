## What this comparison covers

Abyde and Compliancy Group compete directly for the same buyer: a small healthcare practice that needs a structured HIPAA compliance program but does not have an in-house compliance team. This page compares the two on product approach, core features, pricing model, and shared limitations.

This is an independent comparison. Neither vendor sponsors this page.

## Product approach: simplicity vs coaching

The two products take different paths to the same destination.

**Abyde** is built around simplicity. The platform uses a guided compliance wizard designed for practice administrators who are not HIPAA specialists. Abyde has historically targeted dental practices and small medical offices where the administrator wears many hats. The self-guided model keeps costs lower and lets practices work at their own pace. Abyde provides risk assessment, policy templates, training modules, and vendor management tools.

**Compliancy Group** leads with the coaching relationship. Every client gets a dedicated HIPAA Coach who guides the practice through initial setup, risk analysis, and the ongoing compliance calendar. For administrators who want expert guidance rather than software they have to figure out themselves, that coaching model provides real accountability. The coach relationship also means someone is watching for compliance calendar deadlines the practice might miss.

The trade-off is straightforward: Compliancy Group's coaching model costs more than Abyde's self-service approach.

## Pricing

Neither Abyde nor Compliancy Group lists specific pricing on their public pages. Both route buyers through a demo or contact form.

Abyde has historically been positioned at a lower price point, targeting smaller practices that need accessible compliance tools. Compliancy Group's coaching model carries a premium that reflects the dedicated human support included. Contact each vendor for current pricing before making a decision.

One pricing consideration: if your team will spend significant time on self-guided setup and ongoing compliance work, the administrator time cost is real even if the software license is cheaper.

## Dental and specialty practice fit

Abyde is built with dental practices in mind and has deep penetration in the dental market. The interface and terminology reflect dental workflows. Medical practices can use Abyde, but the product framing is dental-first.

Compliancy Group serves both dental and medical practices. The coaching model adapts to both contexts. If you are a general medical practice, neither product has a meaningful fit disadvantage.

## The missing third option

Abyde and Compliancy Group are both compliance binder tools. That is the right description — they help you build and maintain the documentation your compliance program requires: risk analysis, training completion records, policy documents, and vendor agreements.

The shared gap: neither product handles the operational task management that generates real-world audit evidence.

A HIPAA audit does not just review your risk analysis document. Investigators look for evidence of an active compliance program. Who responded to the incident that was reported in March Who followed up after the risk analysis identified a gap in your remote access controls Who made sure the new staff member completed training before their first day of patient contact

Those activities happen in email threads, sticky notes, and verbal reminders — or they do not happen at all. Neither Abyde nor Compliancy Group provides the task management layer where that work gets assigned, tracked, and documented.
