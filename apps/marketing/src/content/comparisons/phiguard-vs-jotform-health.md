## Short answer

Jotform Health and PHIGuard solve different parts of the HIPAA workflow. Jotform Health is for HIPAA-capable form collection and routed submissions. PHIGuard is for the compliance operating record that surrounds forms, intake, incidents, vendors, training, policies, and risk follow-up.

Choose Jotform Health when the problem is collecting patient information through forms. Choose PHIGuard when the problem is running and proving the clinic's HIPAA program.

## Category fit

Jotform's HIPAA materials focus on forms, submissions, encryption, access, and BAA availability for eligible paid accounts. That makes it useful for intake forms, consent forms, internal requests, surveys, and structured collection workflows that may include PHI.

PHIGuard does not try to be the clinic's form builder. It gives the clinic one place to manage what happens after and around those forms: risk analysis, incident triage, vendor BAAs, training evidence, policies, access reviews, and audit history.

## Pricing and procurement

Jotform support materials state HIPAA compliance is not part of the free plan and is tied to eligible paid HIPAA setup. Clinics should verify current Gold or Enterprise eligibility, pricing, submission limits, storage, users, BAA steps, integrations, and whether every connected workflow stays inside covered use.

PHIGuard publishes clinic-level pricing and makes BAA details available during plan review. It should be evaluated as the operating layer for HIPAA follow-through, not as a form-builder replacement.

## The workflow boundary

Forms often create the first piece of PHI in a workflow, but they are not the whole compliance program. Once a submission arrives, the clinic may need to assign follow-up, document an incident, review a vendor, update a policy, or preserve evidence for an audit.

Jotform Health can help collect the data. PHIGuard helps the clinic manage the accountable work that follows.

## What to verify before buying

Before choosing either product, document these answers:

1. Is the clinic buying a form tool or a compliance operating record
2. Which forms will collect PHI
3. Which plan includes HIPAA features and the BAA
4. Where do submissions route after collection
5. Do automations or integrations move PHI to another vendor
6. Where are incidents and risk follow-up documented
7. How will training, policy, vendor BAA, and access-review evidence be exported

## Bottom line

Jotform Health is the stronger choice when the clinic needs HIPAA-capable forms, intake, consent, surveys, and submission routing.

PHIGuard is the stronger choice when the clinic needs HIPAA work to stay assigned, documented, and audit-ready after forms create work. Many clinics can use both: Jotform Health for collection, PHIGuard for compliance operations.
