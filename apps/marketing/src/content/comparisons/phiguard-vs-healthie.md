## Short answer

Healthie and PHIGuard are not substitutes. Healthie is an EHR and practice-management platform for clinical operations. PHIGuard is a HIPAA compliance operating system for the work around clinical operations.

Choose Healthie if the clinic needs scheduling, charting, billing, telehealth, forms, messaging, and patient engagement. Choose PHIGuard if the clinic needs one place to run HIPAA tasks, incidents, policies, training evidence, vendor BAAs, risk follow-up, and audit history.

## Category fit

Healthie's pricing and help materials describe plans for clinicians and groups, with features such as scheduling, payment processing, charting, telehealth, client portal, forms, document storage, messaging, claims, account permissions, and enterprise support. Its security materials discuss HIPAA, BAA availability, and privacy protections.

PHIGuard assumes the clinic already has systems like an EHR or practice-management platform. The problem PHIGuard solves is the compliance layer those systems do not fully cover: the recurring evidence that a clinic has to maintain outside the chart.

## Pricing and procurement

Healthie publishes plan pricing and plan limits for individual clinicians and groups, with monthly and annual options. Clinics should verify current plan details, active-client limits, provider/admin licenses, claims features, enterprise requirements, integrations, BAA terms, and support scope.

PHIGuard publishes clinic-level pricing and makes BAA details available during plan review. Its cost should be compared against the compliance-work burden it removes: separate training spreadsheets, incident files, policy folders, vendor trackers, and risk-remediation lists.

## The compliance distinction

An EHR can be HIPAA-ready for the workflows it handles and still leave the clinic with a separate HIPAA program to run. Staff training, policy acknowledgement, access reviews, vendor BAA reviews, incident triage, and risk remediation often happen outside the patient chart.

That is PHIGuard's lane. It does not replace Healthie. It gives the administrator a compliance record around the clinical system.

## What to verify before buying

Before choosing either product, document these answers:

1. Does the clinic need a clinical operating system or a compliance operating record
2. Which workflows will contain PHI
3. What does the BAA cover
4. Where will HIPAA training evidence live
5. Where will incident notes and breach-risk decisions live
6. How are vendor BAAs reviewed and renewed
7. How will evidence be exported for an audit, management review, or incident

## Bottom line

Healthie is the stronger choice when the clinic needs EHR and practice-management software. That is a real product category and a valid buying need.

PHIGuard is the stronger choice when the clinic already has clinical software and needs HIPAA work to stay assigned, reviewed, and provable. Many clinics can use both: Healthie for clinical operations, PHIGuard for compliance operations.

## Source Posture and Buying Criteria

This comparison should be used as a structured buyer checklist, not as a substitute for current vendor review. The source set for this page is Healthie: Healthie Pricing - Healthie Software Support; Healthie: Healthie Security and Privacy; Healthie: Healthie FAQ. Plan names, BAA availability, add-ons, and support boundaries can change, so the clinic should verify the current contract path before moving PHI or compliance evidence into either product.
