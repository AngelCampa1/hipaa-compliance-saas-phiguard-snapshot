## Short answer

Vanta and PHIGuard both touch HIPAA, but they are not interchangeable. Vanta is an automated compliance and trust platform for organizations that need evidence collection, continuous monitoring, framework mapping, risk management, policies, training, access information, inventory, Trust Center workflows, and buyer-facing compliance proof. PHIGuard is a small-clinic HIPAA operating record.

Choose Vanta if the organization needs automated compliance infrastructure and external trust workflows. Choose PHIGuard if the clinic needs recurring HIPAA work to stay assigned, documented, and reviewable inside daily operations.

## Category fit

Vanta's HIPAA page says it helps business associates meet HIPAA Security and Breach Notification Rule requirements with automated evidence, guided controls, and continuous monitoring. It also emphasizes integrations, evidence reuse across SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, and other frameworks, centralized access information, inventory management, HIPAA-ready policies, security and HIPAA training, AI-powered compliance, and Trust Center workflows.

That is a strong model for health-tech vendors, software companies, and business associates that sell into healthcare and need to prove trust. It is especially valuable when HIPAA sits beside SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, vendor diligence, customer questionnaires, and access reviews.

PHIGuard starts from the covered-entity clinic. The daily problem is not usually framework mapping across cloud controls. It is keeping training, policies, vendor BAAs, incidents, risk follow-up, access changes, and audit evidence from scattering across email and spreadsheets.

## Pricing and procurement

Vanta's pricing page publishes plan structure and asks buyers to request a demo for personalized pricing. It describes Essentials, Plus, Professional, Pro, and Enterprise-style packages with different levels of compliance, AI, questionnaire automation, access management, risk management, reporting, Trust Center, and advanced GRC functionality.

PHIGuard publishes clinic-level pricing and includes a Business Associate Agreement on every public plan. For a small clinic, that makes the buying model easier to compare because the price is tied to the clinic rather than to a broader trust program.

## Where Vanta fits

Vanta fits digital health companies, SaaS business associates, and larger organizations that need automated evidence and customer trust workflows. If a company needs to show buyers a Trust Center, answer questionnaires, reuse evidence across frameworks, monitor cloud systems, and organize audit readiness, Vanta belongs in the shortlist.

That is not the same problem a traditional clinic usually has. A clinic may use an EHR, billing system, intake tools, file storage, and several vendors, but the HIPAA evidence often comes from staff behavior and decisions rather than from cloud-control connectors.

## Where PHIGuard fits

PHIGuard fits when the clinic's core issue is follow-through. Did the new hire complete HIPAA training? Which staff member acknowledged the updated privacy policy? Which vendor needs a BAA review? What happened after a suspected disclosure? Who owns the risk-analysis remediation item?

PHIGuard keeps those answers in one operating record. The clinic does not need to turn every operational question into a GRC configuration project. It needs the work to be assigned, completed, and preserved.

## What to verify before buying

Before choosing either product, document:

1. Whether the buyer is a covered entity clinic or a business associate selling into healthcare.
2. Which frameworks are in scope besides HIPAA.
3. Which systems need automated evidence collection.
4. Whether Trust Center, questionnaires, and buyer-facing reporting are required.
5. How training, policies, vendor BAAs, incidents, and risk follow-up are handled.
6. How BAA terms work for PHI-related use.
7. What current pricing includes for frameworks, integrations, users, add-ons, and support.

## Bottom line

Vanta is a strong platform when the buyer needs automated compliance, framework mapping, trust workflows, and evidence across a technology stack. It is especially relevant for business associates and health-tech companies that need to prove security and compliance to customers.

PHIGuard is the better fit for small clinics that need HIPAA work to happen inside everyday operations. For covered entities, the proof often comes from staff tasks, incidents, vendor records, and risk follow-up. PHIGuard keeps those pieces in one place.
