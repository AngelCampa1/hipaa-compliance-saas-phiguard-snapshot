## Short answer

Accountable HQ and Abyde are both credible HIPAA compliance software options for clinics, but the buying motion differs. Accountable is easier to evaluate when the clinic wants published HIPAA software tiers, included employee counts, and a software-led compliance workflow. Abyde is easier to justify when the clinic wants a healthcare compliance package spanning HIPAA, OSHA, security risk analysis, and support.

Neither comparison should stop at feature lists. A clinic should ask what happens after setup: who owns the remediation task, where incident notes live, how vendor BAAs are reviewed, and whether evidence survives normal staff turnover.

## Category fit

Accountable's current public pricing page frames the product as HIPAA compliance software with Basic HIPAA, Plus, and Pro tiers. Public materials describe policies, security risk assessment, training, incident response, vendor management, BAA management, data inventory, reports, remediation plans, support, and security testing in higher tiers. That is a strong fit for buyers who want a self-contained compliance software program with transparent tiering.

Abyde frames itself as healthcare compliance software for organizations that want HIPAA, OSHA, and security risk analysis options. Its pricing flow asks buyers to select products, locations, employees, and billing cadence. That makes Abyde useful for clinics evaluating multiple healthcare compliance categories at once instead of only HIPAA software.

## Pricing and procurement

Accountable currently publishes specific HIPAA software plan pricing, annual billing totals, included employee counts, extra-seat pricing, trial language, and plan differences. That helps clinics compare total cost before booking a demo. The procurement question is whether the clinic needs Basic HIPAA, Plus monitoring/vendor depth, or Pro support and testing.

Abyde publishes a pricing path, but the final amount depends on products selected and clinic details. That can be useful when HIPAA, OSHA, and security risk analysis are evaluated together. Clinics should request the final annual total for their exact locations, employees, products, and billing term.

## The operational gap to check

HHS risk-analysis guidance makes clear that risk analysis is not just documentation. The clinic has to understand where ePHI is created, received, maintained, or transmitted, then manage risks over time. That means follow-up work has to survive after the assessment is complete.

Accountable and Abyde can both support compliance-program work. The clinic should still verify daily ownership: how tasks are assigned, how incidents are preserved, how vendor reviews recur, how access reviews are documented, and how evidence exports if staff change.

## Where PHIGuard fits

PHIGuard is not included here as a generic "also consider" mention. It solves a different buyer problem: keeping HIPAA obligations connected to recurring operating work. Training evidence, policy acknowledgement, incident notes, vendor BAA review, risk remediation, and audit history live in one system.

That matters when the clinic's problem is not finding a compliance checklist. It is making sure the checklist turns into completed work with owners, dates, and evidence.

## What to verify before buying

Before choosing Accountable HQ or Abyde, document these answers:

1. Which products and modules are included for HIPAA, OSHA, security risk analysis, and vendor management?
2. How do employee count, locations, billing term, extra seats, and support change the price?
3. Does the vendor sign the right BAA for the clinic's use case?
4. How are risk-analysis findings assigned and tracked after the initial assessment?
5. How are incidents documented, escalated, and exported?
6. How are vendor BAAs reviewed, renewed, and linked to systems that receive PHI?
7. Where will daily clinic follow-through and task-level audit history live if it does not live in the compliance platform?

## Bottom line

Choose Accountable HQ when published HIPAA compliance software tiers and a software-led program fit the clinic's buying process. Choose Abyde when the clinic wants a healthcare compliance package that can combine HIPAA, OSHA, security risk analysis, and support.

Choose PHIGuard when the clinic's main risk is operational drift. If HIPAA work needs to stay assigned, reviewed, and provable in daily operations, the operating record matters as much as the initial compliance program.
