## Short answer

Dock Health and PHIGuard both matter to clinics that want HIPAA-safe work management, but they serve different jobs. Dock Health is a healthcare task-management and workflow tool. PHIGuard is a compliance operating system for the HIPAA program itself.

Choose Dock Health when the main problem is routing patient-context tasks across a care team. Choose PHIGuard when the main problem is proving that HIPAA work happened: training, policies, incidents, vendor BAAs, risk follow-up, access reviews, and audit history.

## Category fit

Dock Health positions itself around HIPAA-compliant task management, collaboration, reminders, protocols, templates, and healthcare workflow automation. Its help center includes HIPAA and BAA materials, and its app listing emphasizes healthcare team coordination across administrative and clinical work.

PHIGuard starts from a different place. It assumes the clinic already has clinical systems and task noise. The gap is the HIPAA operating record: who owns a risk remediation item, who acknowledged the policy, which vendor BAA needs review, what happened during an incident, and where the evidence lives.

## Pricing and procurement

Dock's help center points buyers to monthly and annual subscriptions and a trial path. Clinics should confirm current subscription details, user limits, integrations, AI features, BAA steps, and whether every intended workflow is inside covered use.

PHIGuard uses clinic-level pricing with BAA details available during plan review. That matters when the compliance work touches administrators, providers, billers, and staff who all need to contribute evidence without splitting records across tools.

## What to verify before buying

Before choosing either product, document these answers:

1. Is the clinic buying healthcare task routing or HIPAA program operations
2. Which workflows will contain PHI or patient context
3. Does the BAA cover the exact use case and user population
4. Where will risk-analysis remediation tasks live
5. Where will incident notes and follow-up decisions live
6. How will training, policy acknowledgement, and vendor BAA evidence be exported
7. How much history survives if a staff member leaves

## Bottom line

Dock Health has a real lane when a clinic needs healthcare task management, coordination, patient-context workflow, and reminders. It should stay on the shortlist for that job.

PHIGuard is the stronger choice when the clinic needs HIPAA obligations to stay assigned, reviewed, and provable. For compliance work, the operating record is the product.
