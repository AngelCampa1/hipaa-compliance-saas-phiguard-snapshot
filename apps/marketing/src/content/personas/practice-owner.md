You own the clinic, so you own the risk. If a regulator finds a problem, the fine lands on the business. If patient data leaks, the cost and the trust loss are yours. HIPAA is not paperwork to you. It is exposure.

PHI means Protected Health Information, the patient data HIPAA protects. A BAA is a Business Associate Agreement, the contract a vendor signs before they can touch that data. An audit trail is a saved record of who did what and when.

PHIGuard lowers that risk at a price you can plan for.

## The risk an owner carries

Small clinics get audited too. A patient complaint, a lost laptop, or a vendor breach can bring the Office for Civil Rights to your door. When they come, they ask for proof that you ran a real program.

Most clinics cannot show it. The work was done, but the record is scattered or missing. That gap is what turns a small problem into a big fine.

## What PHIGuard gives you

PHIGuard runs the compliance program and keeps the proof.

Risk analysis, staff training, policies, access checks, vendor BAAs, and incident response all live as tracked work. Each task has an owner and a due date. When it is done, the date is saved.

Every action is written to an audit trail you cannot edit. If a regulator asks, you show the history instead of scrambling. That record is your protection.

## Why flat pricing protects your margin

Per-seat tools punish growth. Every hire raises the bill. For an owner watching margin, that is a tax on hiring.

PHIGuard charges one flat price per clinic. Annual billing lowers the effective rate. Add the whole team without watching the cost climb.

One tool runs the whole program. You do not stitch together five apps and pay for each.

## A simple way to think about it

A HIPAA fine can run into the tens of thousands or more. A breach can cost far beyond that. PHIGuard costs a flat monthly rate and gives you a record that lowers the odds of both.

That is the trade. A known, small cost against a large, unknown one.
