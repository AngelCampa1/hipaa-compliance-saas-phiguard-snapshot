You do not run one clinic. You run several. The same training, the same policies, and the same risk reviews have to happen at every site. The hard part is seeing it all at once and knowing which location is behind.

PHI means Protected Health Information, the patient data HIPAA protects. A BAA is a Business Associate Agreement, the contract a vendor signs before they can touch that data. An audit trail is a saved record of who did what and when.

PHIGuard gives you one place to run the whole group.

## The job across locations

When you manage many sites, compliance spreads thin. One clinic finishes training on time. Another forgets. A third logged an incident but never closed it out. You find out too late, usually when something goes wrong.

The problem is visibility. You cannot run what you cannot see. Chasing five separate spreadsheets does not give you a clear picture.

## What PHIGuard gives you

PHIGuard is built for the group, not just one site.

You roll out the same tasks to every location at once. Training, policies, risk reviews, access checks, and vendor BAAs all push out as tracked work. Each site has its own owners and due dates. You see which locations are on track and which are behind, in one view.

Every action is written to an audit trail. Each location keeps its own record, and you get a view across the whole group. During a review, you can show one site's history or the group's history.

PHIGuard does not replace the EHR at each clinic. It handles the compliance work across all of them.

## One rollout, every site

The slow part of multi-site compliance is doing the same setup over and over. PHIGuard removes that.

You build the task once and send it to every location. New site? It inherits the same program on day one. You stop rebuilding the wheel at each clinic.

## Why flat pricing fits a group

Per-seat tools get expensive fast across many sites and many staff. The bill climbs with every hire at every location.

PHIGuard charges one flat price per clinic. The cost stays clear as you add sites and staff.
