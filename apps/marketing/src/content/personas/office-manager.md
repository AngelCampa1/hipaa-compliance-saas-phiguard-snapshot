You already run a lot. The front desk, the schedule, the staff, the supply order that was supposed to ship last week. Then HIPAA gets added on top. Training, onboarding, sign-offs, and the records to prove it all happened.

PHI means Protected Health Information, the patient data HIPAA protects. A BAA is a Business Associate Agreement, the contract a vendor signs before they can touch that data. An audit trail is a saved record of who did what and when.

PHIGuard puts the HIPAA work into the routine you already run.

## The work that lands on the office manager

In most small clinics, the office manager is the one who makes HIPAA happen day to day. You train the new hire. You chase the sign-off. You keep the proof in a folder somewhere.

The problem is not the work itself. The problem is that it lives in too many places. Training in one inbox. Onboarding in a paper packet. Sign-offs in a binder. When you need to show it was done, you go digging.

## What PHIGuard gives you

PHIGuard works the way you already work. You assign tasks. You set due dates. You check what is done.

Staff training, policy sign-offs, access reviews, and new-hire steps all live as tracked tasks. Each one has a real owner. When it is done, the date and the person are saved for you.

Every action is written to an audit trail you cannot edit. You stop keeping a second record. The product keeps it.

PHIGuard does not replace your scheduling tool or your EHR. It handles the compliance work around them.

## Onboarding made simple

New hires are where small clinics slip. Someone starts on Monday and touches patient data before training is done. That is a real risk.

PHIGuard gives you a new-hire checklist you assign on day one. It covers training, access setup, and policy sign-off. Each step is tracked and saved. You can show the work was done before anyone touched PHI.

## Why flat pricing helps you

Per-seat tools charge more every time you hire. For a clinic that runs on part-time and seasonal staff, that adds up fast.

PHIGuard charges one flat price per clinic. Add the whole team without watching the bill climb.
