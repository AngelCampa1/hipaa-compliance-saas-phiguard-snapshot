## How to Use This Template

This package covers the full HIPAA complaint lifecycle: intake, acknowledgment, investigation, outcome, and corrective action. Adapt each section to your clinic's letterhead and internal process. Every complaint you receive must be documented — including complaints you determine are unfounded. Absence of documentation is itself a compliance finding.

Keep all completed complaint files in a secure location. The six-year retention requirement at 45 CFR §164.530(j) applies to complaint records. Assign each new complaint a unique ID (e.g., PRIV-2026-001) and file all related documents under that ID.

## Section 2 — Patient Acknowledgment Letter

Send this letter within five business days of receiving the complaint. Adapt to your clinic's letterhead.

---

**[DATE]**

**[PATIENT/COMPLAINANT NAME]**
[ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

**Re: Acknowledgment of Your Privacy Complaint — Complaint ID [PRIV-YEAR-SEQUENCE]**

Dear [NAME]:

Thank you for contacting [CLINIC NAME] regarding a concern about the privacy of your health information. We take patient privacy seriously and are committed to addressing your complaint promptly and thoroughly.

We have received your complaint and assigned it the reference number above. A member of our Privacy team has been designated to review this matter. We will keep you informed of the outcome of our review.

If you have additional information or documentation related to your complaint, please send it to:

**[PRIVACY OFFICER NAME], Privacy Officer**
[CLINIC NAME]
[ADDRESS]
[PHONE] | [EMAIL]

You also have the right to file a complaint directly with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights (OCR) at any time. Information on filing with OCR is available at hhs.gov/hipaa or by calling 1-800-368-1019. Filing a complaint with us does not waive that right.

Sincerely,

[PRIVACY OFFICER NAME]
Privacy Officer, [CLINIC NAME]

**Summary of our findings:**

[Choose one of the following and adapt as appropriate:]

**Option A — Complaint substantiated:**
Our investigation found that [describe the issue found]. We apologize for this occurrence. We have taken the following corrective action: [describe action]. We have also put additional safeguards in place to prevent a recurrence.

**Option B — Complaint not substantiated:**
Our investigation did not find evidence that a privacy violation occurred. [Provide a brief factual explanation of what was reviewed and what was found.] We nonetheless appreciate you bringing this matter to our attention.

**Option C — Inconclusive:**
Our investigation was unable to conclusively determine whether the described incident occurred. We have taken the following precautionary steps: [describe steps].

If you have additional information that may be relevant to this matter, please contact our Privacy Officer at the information below. You retain the right to file a complaint with HHS OCR at any time.
