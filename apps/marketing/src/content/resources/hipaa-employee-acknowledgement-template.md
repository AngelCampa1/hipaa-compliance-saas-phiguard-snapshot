## Who Needs This Template

Practice administrators who onboard new clinical or administrative staff need a signed acknowledgement packet before granting PHI access. This is not optional. Under the HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR §164.530(b)) and Security Rule (45 CFR §164.308(a)(5)), covered entities must train workforce members on applicable policies and procedures and document that training. Under §164.308(a)(1)(ii)(C), covered entities must apply sanctions to workforce members who violate security policies - and those sanctions must be documented in a written policy the workforce member has acknowledged.

Without signed acknowledgements on file, a clinic cannot demonstrate compliance with these requirements during an OCR investigation.

## What HIPAA Rule It Satisfies

Three HIPAA provisions require written acknowledgements from workforce members:

**45 CFR §164.530(b) - Privacy Rule training.** Covered entities must train all workforce members on the clinic's privacy policies and procedures no later than the compliance date, when functions change, and within a reasonable period of a new hire's joining. Training must be documented.

**45 CFR §164.530(e) - Privacy Rule sanction policy.** Covered entities must have a sanctions policy that applies appropriate sanctions against workforce members who violate the clinic's privacy policies and procedures. The sanctions policy must be documented in writing, and workforce members must acknowledge it.

**45 CFR §164.308(a)(1)(ii)(C) - Security Rule sanctions policy.** Covered entities must apply appropriate sanctions to workforce members who fail to comply with security policies or procedures. This Security Rule parallel to §164.530(e) applies specifically to ePHI security obligations. Workforce members must acknowledge the security sanctions policy separately or in a combined document that addresses both.

The acknowledgement packet in this template satisfies all three requirements in a single document set.

## What Is Inside This Template

**Document 1 - HIPAA Privacy Training Acknowledgement.** States that the workforce member has received training on the clinic's HIPAA Privacy Policy, the Notice of Privacy Practices, patient rights under the Privacy Rule, and the minimum-necessary standard. Includes: employee name, role, hire date, signature, date of training, training method (in-person, online module, video), trainer name, and the version number or effective date of the Privacy Policy acknowledged. Capturing the policy version is essential - it proves which version of the policy was in effect when training was delivered, which matters during an OCR investigation if policies have been updated since.

**Document 2 - HIPAA Security Training Acknowledgement.** States that the workforce member has received training on the clinic's HIPAA Security Policy, password and authentication procedures, device handling and screen lock requirements, phishing and social engineering awareness, and incident reporting procedures. Includes a separate signature block and the date of training.

**Document 3 - Sanction Policy Acknowledgement.** The workforce member acknowledges reading and understanding the clinic's Sanction Policy, and understands that violations of HIPAA privacy and security policies may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, and that certain violations may trigger external reporting obligations under 45 CFR §§164.400-414. Includes date and supervisor countersignature line.

**Document 4 - Confidentiality Obligations Statement.** Covers PHI confidentiality obligations during and after employment, prohibition on unauthorized disclosure, prohibition on PHI access for personal purposes, and the reporting obligation for suspected incidents. Explicitly states that obligations survive termination.

**Document 5 - PHI Handling Procedures Acknowledgement.** Role-specific acknowledgement (clinical or administrative version) that the workforce member has reviewed the clinic's PHI handling, storage, transmission, and disposal procedures. References the clinic's specific systems and procedures by name.

**Personnel File Checklist.** A checklist for the administrator to confirm all five documents are signed, dated, and filed before PHI access is provisioned.

## How to Use This Template

**Step 1.** Prepare all five documents with the clinic name, employee name, role, and hire date before the first day.

**Step 2.** Deliver training before obtaining signatures. Acknowledgements signed before training is delivered are a compliance gap - the signature must attest to training already received.

**Step 3.** Complete each signature block on the day of training. Do not send home for later signature.

**Step 4.** File all five signed documents in the personnel file before the employee's first PHI access.

**Step 5.** Repeat the Security Training Acknowledgement annually. PHI handling and sanction policy acknowledgements should be re-obtained any time those policies are materially updated.

## Who Fills It Out, Where to Store It, and When to Re-Obtain It

**Who fills it out:** The practice administrator or designated Privacy/Security Officer prepares the packet. The workforce member signs each document on the first day of employment before PHI access is provisioned. A supervisor or administrator countersigns the Sanction Policy Acknowledgement.

**Where to store it:** All signed acknowledgements go into the personnel file - not the patient record, not a shared drive, and not in email threads. Retain for six years from the date of creation or last effective date per 45 CFR §164.530(j). For Security Rule acknowledgements, also retain per §164.316(b)(2)(i). When a workforce member is terminated, move the file to a secure archive - the retention obligation continues.

**How often it is re-obtained:** The Security Training Acknowledgement (Document 2) should be re-obtained annually as part of the required security awareness training cycle under §164.308(a)(5). The Sanction Policy Acknowledgement and Privacy Training Acknowledgement should be re-obtained any time those policies are materially updated, since the prior signature attests only to the version that was in effect at signing.

**What triggers an immediate re-acknowledgement:** Any material update to the Privacy Policy, Security Policy, or Sanction Policy requires re-distribution and re-acknowledgement from all workforce members within a reasonable period. A workforce member's change of role that gives them access to additional categories of PHI also requires role-specific PHI handling acknowledgement (Document 5) to be re-obtained for the new role.
