## How to Use This Log

This log documents that your clinic conducted HIPAA training, who attended, what was covered, and when each staff member signed their attestation. Print or complete it digitally. Keep the completed original in your compliance records for at least six years from the training date, per 45 CFR §164.530(j).

Run a separate log for each training session. If your clinic conducts annual all-staff training on one day, one log covers the session. If you train staff in smaller groups across several dates, run a log for each date. New hires who complete onboarding training should be documented on a separate new-hire training log, not folded into the annual log — this makes it clear what training was initial versus refresher.

When OCR investigates a small clinic, training documentation is one of the first things requested. Investigators look for: who was trained, on what date, what the training covered, and whether every workforce member is accounted for. This log is designed to answer every one of those questions.

## Part 5 — Retention Reminder and Filing Instructions

**Retention requirement:** Retain this completed training log, including the signed attendance table and topics checklist, for a minimum of **six years** from the training date. Retain indefinitely any log that is the only documentation of a staff member's initial training.

**Where to file:** Store training logs in your compliance records folder, organized by year. Digital files should be named: [YYYY]-[MM]-[DD]-HIPAA-Training-Log.pdf (or equivalent). Paper logs should be stored in a locked file cabinet.

**What OCR asks for in an audit:**

When OCR investigates a covered entity, common document requests include:
- Training policy (how training is conducted, how often, which staff)
- Training logs for the past several years (typically 3–6 years)
- Training materials used (curriculum, slides, or online module documentation)
- Evidence that new hires received training within a reasonable period
- Documentation showing training was updated when policies changed

This log, combined with your training materials and a training policy that describes your approach, satisfies all of these requests. File all three together so they are easy to produce.
